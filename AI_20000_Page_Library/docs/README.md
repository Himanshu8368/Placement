- **Reading progress bar** - Track your reading position

- **Reading history** - See your recently read pages (last 3 on homepage)

## 📖 How to Use

1. **Browse by Collection**: Use the top navigation tabs or the links above to choose AI, DSA, or System Design
2. **Search**: Use the search bar at the top to find specific topics (or press `/`)
3. **Direct Links**: Inside each collection, use the sidebar to jump directly to sections and chapters
4. **Bookmark Pages**: Click the bookmark button to save pages for later
5. **View Related Content**: Scroll to the bottom of any article to see related pages
6. **Learn Terms**: Hover over highlighted terms to see glossary definitions
7. **Track Progress**: See your reading history on the homepage
8. **Keyboard Shortcuts**: Press `?` to see all available shortcuts
