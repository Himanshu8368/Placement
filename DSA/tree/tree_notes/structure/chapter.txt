CHAPTER TEMPLATE                                       target 35-50 pages
================

Chapter N: <Title>
|
+-- chNN.tex  (master; replaces the outline stub)
|     \bookchapter{<Title>}{cNN}
|     \chapterinfo{LC Easy--Medium}{Written}   (Outline while not written)
|     \input{chapters/chNN_slug/00_opener}
|     \input{chapters/chNN_slug/01_<topic>}
|     \input{...}            02..0K_<topic>   (K = as many topics as the chapter
|     needs; 3-6 is typical -- do NOT force a fixed count)
|     \input{chapters/chNN_slug/99_chapter_end}
|     \closechapter
|
+-- 00_opener.tex  (1-2 pages; NO numbered boxes: they would be numbered N.0.x)
|     chaptertheorybox{Chapter at a Glance}
|         what the chapter is about and why (3-5 sentences)
|         "You will learn to" (4-6 bullets, concrete skills)
|         Prerequisites (\chref / \secref)
|         In contests / interviews (where it shows up)
|     figure[H] roadmap: one node per topic with 2-3 key results;
|         arrows = dependencies; \caption*{...}
|     table: Topic | Key results | Solved problems (\secref, \probref)
|     \subsection*{Notation Used in This Chapter}  small 4-column table
|
+-- 01..08 topic files            -> topic.txt(decide how much and which topic is needed in this chapter)
|
+-- 99_chapter_end.tex            -> chapter_end.txt

NOTES
- Topics follow the outline in the stub (\plannedtopic entries): keep its concept
  lists and notes. Reorder topics only if a later topic is needed to prove an earlier
  one, and say so in the chNN.tex header and the opener caption.
- Problem budget per topic: at least 2 fully solved (usually 3) + a practice list
  (the outline's planned count minus the solved ones, at least 4).
- Every tree gets drawn. If a topic mentions a shape, a figure shows it.
- The outline may mark concepts "(added)": they are part of the chapter.
