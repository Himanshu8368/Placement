\chapter{From Constraints to Algorithm}
\label{ch:constraints}

Binary search announces itself in two ways: a huge answer range with a cheap check, or $q$ queries on sorted data. Read $n$, the range of the answer $R$ and $q$, and look the budget up here. A judge runs roughly $10^8$ simple operations per second; $\log_2 10^{18} \approx 60$.

\section*{Step 1 --- What the limits allow}

\begingroup\small\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.3}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}l l X@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Limit} & \textbf{Budget} & \textbf{Typical binary search} \\
\midrule
$q \le 2\cdot10^5$ queries on sorted data & $\bigO{q \log n}$ & bounds, counting, floor and ceiling (Ch.~\ref{ch:bounds}) \\
$n \le 2\cdot10^5$, answer up to $10^9$ & $\bigO{n \log R}$ & binary search on the answer with a linear check (Ch.~\ref{ch:answer_intro}--\ref{ch:maximin}) \\
answer up to $10^{18}$ & $\approx 60$ checks & time and budget answers; watch overflow in the check (Ch.~\ref{ch:time_budget}) \\
$n \le 10^5$, $k$-th of $n^2$ values & $\bigO{n \log n \log R}$ & value search with a counting check (Ch.~\ref{ch:kth}) \\
real answer, precision $10^{-6}$ & 60--100 iterations & real binary search, ternary search (Ch.~\ref{ch:real}, \ref{ch:ternary}) \\
$q$ independent searches over one process & $\bigO{(n+q) \log R}$ & offline / parallel binary search (Ch.~\ref{ch:offline}) \\
hidden array, $\lceil\log_2 n\rceil$ queries & --- & interactive binary search (Ch.~\ref{ch:interactive}) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\endgroup

\begin{insightbox}[Count the checks]
Binary search over $[0, 10^{18}]$ costs 60 checks. If one check is $\bigO{n}$ with $n = 2\cdot10^5$, that is $1.2\cdot10^7$ operations: comfortable. If the check itself contains a $\log$ (sorting, a set), multiply again --- usually still fine; a third $\log$ rarely is.
\end{insightbox}

\begin{warningbox}[Monotone first, code second]
Every bug in binary search on the answer comes from a predicate that is not monotone or from bounds that are not safe. Prove both on paper before writing the loop.
\end{warningbox}
